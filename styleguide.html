Surfaces and elevation

Base, raised, and floating surfaces describe material. Elevation describes depth, independently of layout, radius, and stacking order. Components consume variables directly.

Base

Opaque page surface; no default shadow.

Raised

The sticky topbar uses medium elevation with its own background, border, and blur tokens, initially sharing the floating material.

Floating

Shared translucent background, border, blur, and large elevation for the mini-player, notifications, action sheets, and desktop player dialog.

--elevation-sm

Shallow depth: hovered and pressed tiles.

--elevation-md

Resting tiles and artwork.

--elevation-lg

Floating depth, also usable independently of material.
