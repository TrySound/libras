Token structure

Foundations define raw values; semantic roles describe their purpose. Components consume semantic colors, typography, and surfaces directly, while keeping local configuration scoped.

Semantic roles

Text, surfaces, borders, controls, status colors, and typography.

--color-text-accent · --type-body-size

Page and card surfaces use --surface-page-background and --surface-card-background. Control fills use --color-control-*; inactive switch and progress tracks use --color-track, independently of borders. Artwork colors share the --color-artwork-* prefix.

Effects and semantic surfaces

Surface backgrounds and borders reference the palette directly, without intermediate color aliases. Shadows reuse elevation tokens. Raised and floating surfaces currently match in material but remain independently tunable and differ in depth.

--elevation-* · --surface-*
Component configuration

Control sizes and colors stay local. Dialog offsets are local, and swipe offsets are supplied at runtime.

--control-* · --swipe-offset

Surfaces and elevation

Page, card, input, accent, danger, raised, and floating surfaces describe material. Each defines only the background, border, or effects it needs. Elevation describes depth, independently of layout, radius, and stacking order. These are tokens, not surface classes.

Control typography

Buttons and inputs consume their typography tokens through .button and .input.

Inputs

Use .input to opt text controls into shared typography, surface, and focus styles. Hover to highlight the border; use Tab to see the focus outline. Disabled inputs are dimmed and ignore hover. Invalid borders use :user-invalid or aria-invalid="true", so untouched required fields are not marked as errors. Switches and sliders keep their own styles.

Switches

Native checkboxes styled with .switch. Click or press Space to toggle. Like buttons, enabled switches brighten on hover and dim and shrink when pressed. Reduced motion disables animation and scaling; disabled switches have no feedback.

Cards

Use .card for a padded surface. Compose its contents with stack and row utilities; add .grow beside actions and .truncate only to text that should stay on one line. Cards are neutral by default; data-variant="danger" adds error colors. Add role="alert" only for dynamic messages requiring immediate announcement, not static examples.

Track lists

Albums, tracks, and queue items share synchronized columns. Empty overlay links or buttons make each row clickable; secondary links and controls remain above the overlay. Track numbers are padded to at least two digits. Album tracks show an artist credit only when it differs from the album artist; queue rows show the artist without an album name. Durations are right-aligned and omitted when unknown.
